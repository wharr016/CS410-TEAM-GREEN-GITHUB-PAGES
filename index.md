## Project: Volunteer Hub
- Volunteer Hub is a mobile and web app that connects volunteers who are looking for opportunities to give back with organizations that need help. It allows volunteers to search for opportunities by location and/or distance and sign up individually or as a team. Organizations can post opportunities and manage their volunteers.
